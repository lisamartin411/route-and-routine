International Relocation

Personalised planning and research to help you organise your move abroad.

International relocation, planned around you

Relocating to another country can involve a huge amount of research, paperwork and practical planning, with requirements that can vary significantly depending on where you're moving. Whether you're relocating alone, as a couple or with your family, support can be tailored around your destination, circumstances and priorities, helping bring the many moving parts of an international move into one clear and manageable plan.

What we can help with:

  • Visa and immigration requirement research
  • Residency and registration requirements
  • Required documentation and application information
  • Driving and licensing requirements
  • Housing and accommodation research
  • International removal company and shipping research
  • Packing and moving organisation
  • Insurance research and requirements
  • Utilities and household services research
  • Banking and everyday financial administration
  • Healthcare registration research
  • Creating a digital filing system for relocation and home paperwork

These are just some examples of what we can help with. Every package is tailored to your individual requirements, so your final package will be built around what you need help with.
