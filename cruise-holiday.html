Cruise Holiday Planning

Personalised planning and support to help you organise your cruise holiday.

Planning a cruise involves more than choosing a ship and destination. From finding the right itinerary and cabin to organising excursions, transfers and the practical details around your trip, planning can be tailored around your preferences, budget and priorities to help you make the most of your cruise holiday.

What we can help with:

  • Cruise line and ship research
  • Cruise itinerary research and comparison
  • Cabin research and comparison
  • Flights and transport options
  • Airport and port transfers
  • Excursion and shore experience research
  • Pre- and post-cruise accommodation
  • Restaurants and dining options
  • Itinerary planning
  • Budget planning
  • Travel insurance information
  • Passport and visa requirements

These are just some examples of what we can help with. Every package is tailored to your individual requirements, so your final package will be built around what you need help with.

Only need a little help? You don't need to book a full package. Individual tasks or smaller pieces of research can also be arranged and priced separately, so you can get help with exactly what you need.
