Hen & Stag Party Planning

Personalised planning and support to help you organise your celebration.

Planning a hen or stag party often means coordinating different preferences, budgets, schedules and logistics across a group. Whether you're planning a relaxed day out, a full on day of activities or something completely different, planning can be tailored around the group, the guest of honour and the type of celebration you have in mind to help you bring the whole celebration together while keeping everyone organised.

What we can help with:

  • Activity and experience research
  • Adventure and adrenaline activities
  • Spa and wellness experiences
  • Restaurants and group dining
  • Bars and nightlife
  • Entertainment
  • Themed activities and experiences
  • Group-friendly attractions and days out
  • Guest and group organisation
  • Itinerary and timings
  • Budget planning
  • Splitting costs and keeping track of group arrangements

These are just some examples of what we can help with. Every package is tailored to your individual requirements, so your final package will be built around what you need help with.
