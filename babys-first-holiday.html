Baby's First Holiday Planning

Personalised planning and support to help you feel prepared for your first holiday with your baby.

Planning your first holiday with a baby comes with a whole new set of things to consider. From choosing somewhere suitable to travel with a baby through to packing, getting around and planning what you'll need while you're away, support can be tailored around your family and the level of help you need, making your first family holiday feel more manageable.

What we can help with:

  • Baby-friendly destination research
  • Accommodation research
  • Flights and transport options
  • Baby-friendly facilities and amenities
  • Travel and airport considerations
  • Baby packing checklist
  • Essential items to take
  • Travel equipment recommendations
  • Baby-friendly activities and experiences
  • Transfers and getting around
  • Travel insurance information
  • Practical tips for travelling with a baby

These are just some examples of what we can help with. Every package is tailored to your individual requirements, so your final package will be built around what you need help with.

From £85

Only need a little help? You don't need to book a full package. Individual tasks or smaller pieces of research can also be arranged and priced separately, so you can get help with exactly what you need.
