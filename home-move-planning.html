Home Move Planning

Personalised planning and support to help you organise your move.

Moving home involves a lot of practical details, deadlines and arrangements that can be difficult to keep track of alongside everyday life. Whether you're moving locally or simply want help taking control of the admin, support can be tailored around your circumstances, priorities and how much help you need to stay on top of the move from preparation through to settling in.

What we can help with:

  • Removal company research and comparison
  • Storage research and arrangements
  • Repairs and outstanding jobs before moving
  • Utilities and bills setup
  • Supplier research and comparison
  • Setting up or transferring utility accounts
  • Cancelling previous accounts
  • Change of address
  • Home insurance research and policy comparison
  • Appliance warranties, manuals and receipts
  • Household accounts and outstanding admin
  • Creating a digital filing system for important home paperwork

These are just some examples of what we can help with. Every package is tailored to your individual requirements, so your final package will be built around what you need help with.

From £175

Only need a little help? You don't need to book a full package. Individual tasks or smaller pieces of research can also be arranged and priced separately, so you can get help with exactly what you need.

Not sure how much help you need? Get in touch and we'll recommend the most suitable package for you and the stage of your move you're at.
