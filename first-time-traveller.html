First-Time Traveller Help

Personalised planning and support to help you feel prepared and confident for your first trip.

Your first trip doesn't have to feel daunting. Whether you're unsure where to start, need help understanding the practicalities of travelling, or simply want reassurance that you've covered everything, support can be tailored around your destination and individual needs, giving you the practical guidance and personalised information you need to feel prepared and travel with confidence.

What we can help with:

  • Destination research
  • Accommodation research
  • Flights and transport options
  • Passport and visa requirements
  • Travel insurance guidance
  • Airport and flight preparation
  • Personalised packing checklist
  • Attractions and things to do
  • Restaurant recommendations
  • Local customs and tipping information
  • Currency and spending guidance
  • Local emergency information

The above is just a selection of what your package can include. Every package is tailored to your individual trip, and the exact planning and research provided will depend on your requirements.

From £75

Only need a little help? You don't need to book a full package. Individual tasks or smaller pieces of research can also be arranged and priced separately, so you can get help with exactly what you need.

Not sure how much help you need? Get in touch and we'll recommend the most suitable package for your trip.
