3. Information We May Collect

We only collect personal information that you choose to provide to us when you:

  • contact us through our website
  • enquire about our services
  • become a client
  • communicate with us during the course of providing a service

Depending on your enquiry or the services you receive, this information may include:

  • your name
  • your email address
  • the type of service you are enquiring about
  • details about your enquiry or requirements
  • information you include in your correspondence with us
  • documents or information you choose to provide that are relevant to the services requested

Please only provide information that is relevant to your enquiry.

We ask that you do not include sensitive personal information (such as medical information, passport details or financial information) in your initial enquiry unless we specifically ask you to do so.

In some circumstances, limited sensitive information may become necessary in order to provide a requested service. For example, information relating to accessibility requirements or other travel-related needs may be relevant when assisting with travel planning. Where this information is required, we will explain why we need it and will only use it for the purpose for which it was provided.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to:

  • respond to enquiries and requests for information
  • understand your requirements and discuss how we may be able to support you
  • communicate with you about your enquiry or service
  • provide the services you have requested
  • organise and manage client projects
  • prepare quotations, proposals, itineraries, planning documents or other materials where applicable
  • maintain appropriate business records
  • comply with legal or regulatory obligations where required

We will only use your personal information for purposes that are necessary, relevant and appropriate to the services we provide, unless we are legally required to use it for another purpose.

5. How We Store and Protect Your Information

We take reasonable steps to keep your personal information secure and protect it from loss, misuse, unauthorised access, alteration or disclosure.

Information may be stored using secure digital services, including email and cloud storage providers used to operate our business and provide our services.

We take appropriate measures to protect access to these systems, including using secure passwords and maintaining suitable account security practices.

Access to personal information is limited to Route & Routine and will only be used where necessary to provide our services, manage our business, or fulfil our legal obligations.

6. How Long We Keep Your Information

We will only retain your personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to deal with your enquiry, provide our services and maintain appropriate business records.

Where information is no longer required, it will normally be deleted within 24 months unless we are required by law to keep it for longer.

If you become a client, we may retain relevant information for longer where necessary for business records, accounting requirements, legal obligations or the protection of our legitimate interests.

Where third-party providers process personal information on our behalf, they do so in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable data protection requirements.

We take reasonable steps to ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place when using third-party services to protect your personal information.

10. Your Data Protection Rights

Under UK data protection laws, you may have the right to:

  • request access to the personal information we hold about you
