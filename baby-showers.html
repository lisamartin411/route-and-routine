Baby Shower Planning

Personalised planning and support to help you organise a thoughtful baby shower.

Planning a baby shower involves bringing together the venue, guests, food, activities and all the little details that make the occasion feel special. Whether you're planning an intimate gathering or a larger celebration, planning can be tailored around the guest of honour, helping you create a thoughtful celebration that everyone can enjoy.

What we can help with:

  • Baby shower venue research
  • Restaurant, café and private dining options
  • Decorations and supplies
  • Catering and food options
  • Baby shower games and activities
  • Entertainment
  • Guest list and invitation organisation
  • Photography
  • Flowers and personalised touches
  • Budget planning
  • Itinerary and timings
  • Surprise shower planning

The above is just a selection of what your package can include. Every package is tailored to your individual trip, and the exact planning and research provided will depend on your requirements.
