First Home Help

Personalised planning and support to help you get organised, settled and on top of the administration that comes with your first home.

Buying your first home is a major milestone, but getting everything organised once the keys are yours can feel like another job in itself. Whether you need help getting the essentials set up, preparing for the move or getting your new-home administration under control, support can be tailored around your priorities and what you need to get settled.

What we can help with:

  • Utilities and bills setup
  • Direct debit setup and payment dates
  • Keeping a record of suppliers, accounts and important details
  • Change of address
  • Home insurance research and policy comparison
  • Pre-move planning, including key dates and deadlines
  • Removal arrangements and storage
  • Repairs and outstanding jobs
  • New-home setup and settling in
  • Checking meter readings, utilities, heating and hot water
  • Organising household accounts and ongoing home administration
  • Creating a digital filing system for your home paperwork

These are just some examples of what we can help with. Every package is tailored to your individual requirements, so your final package will be built around what you need help with.

From £150

Only need a little help? You don't need to book a full package. Individual tasks or smaller pieces of research can also be arranged and priced separately, so you can get help with exactly what you need.
