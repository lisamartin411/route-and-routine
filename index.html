Services

Two focused areas to simplify your life

Route

Whether you’re planning a honeymoon, family getaway, anniversary escape, special trip or simply need help narrowing down options, travel planning can quickly become overwhelming. Support can include destination research, itinerary creation, booking coordination, travel recommendations and organising the details that turn ideas into memorable experiences.

Meaningful moments deserve to feel exciting rather than stressful. Whether celebrating a proposal, anniversary, birthday, baby shower, hen party or intimate occasion, support is available to coordinate the details and create experiences that feel personal and effortless.

Planning for multiple people often means balancing preferences, schedules and logistics. From family trips and weekend itineraries to shared celebrations and group experiences, organisation and coordination help create smoother experiences with less stress.

Routine

Busy schedules can quickly become difficult to manage when appointments, commitments and routines live in different places. Support includes calendar organisation, appointment coordination and family scheduling to create more visibility, reduce mental load and make everyday life feel more manageable.

Running a household involves constant decisions, admin and practical organisation behind the scenes. Whether staying on top of household organisation or coordinating a house move, support helps create structure and reduce overwhelm.

Keeping on top of budgets, recurring payments, paperwork and financial admin can often feel overwhelming alongside everything else. Support focuses on creating structure and organisation around household finances and admin, helping everything feel clearer and easier to manage without providing financial advice.

About Me

Hi, I’m Lisa

After becoming a mum, I started thinking about how I could build something that allows me to be present for my family while continuing to use the skills I’ve developed throughout my career.

I’ve worked in aviation since 2015 on both short-haul and long-haul operations, where organisation, planning, adaptability, and attention to detail are essential every day.

Since 2021, I’ve also worked as a software developer within the aviation industry, strengthening my problem-solving skills, organisation, project management, and ability to manage complex information and systems.

Alongside my professional experience, I’ve always genuinely loved planning. Whether it’s researching destinations, building travel itineraries, organising special occasions, coordinating logistics, or creating systems that make life easier, it’s something I naturally enjoy.

That’s why I’ve decided to start a business helping busy professionals, couples, and families reclaim their time through life admin support, travel planning, and special occasion coordination.

Whether you need help planning a honeymoon, arranging a special trip, managing an overwhelming to-do list, coordinating bookings, budget organisation or simply bringing some order to a busy schedule, I’m here to help.

This is the start of an exciting new chapter, and I’d love for you to follow along on the journey.

About Route & Routine

Route & Routine was created to help make life feel lighter.

When schedules are full, plans become complicated and the to-do list keeps growing, it’s easy to feel stretched in too many directions.

Route & Routine offers thoughtful support with travel planning, special occasions and everyday life admin — helping take the organisation, research and coordination off your plate.

The goal is simple: create more time, reduce stress, and bring calm and clarity back to the things that matter most.

Because sometimes you don’t need to do more — you just need someone to help carry the load.

Need help with something that isn’t listed? Please get in touch anyway — every situation is different, and sometimes the best place to start is simply a conversation.
